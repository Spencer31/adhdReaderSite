Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

This policy explains what information ADHD Reader stores or processes, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information stored on your device

ADHD Reader stores your app settings, reading progress, elapsed reading time, and completed-reading achievements on your device. This information lets the app remember your preferences and help you continue a story. ADHD Reader does not require you to create an account.

Narration

ADHD Reader uses Apple’s built-in speech synthesis to read story text aloud with a voice installed on your device. ADHD Reader does not record your voice or request microphone access. Available voices and voice downloads are managed through your device’s Apple settings.

Purchases

ADHD Reader offers in-app purchases and subscriptions through Apple’s StoreKit. Apple processes payment information and provides ADHD Reader with purchase and entitlement status so the app can unlock content and restore purchases. ADHD Reader does not receive your full payment-card details. Apple’s handling of purchase information is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Usage analytics

ADHD Reader uses Amplitude only when you enable Share Anonymous Usage in the app’s Settings screen. Analytics helps us understand app reliability and how features are used. It may include app sessions; screens viewed; interactions with reading packs, narration, settings, purchases, and paywalls; reading progress and timing; pack and story identifiers; item counts; selected word-limit and image settings; and the app version, build number, and environment.

ADHD Reader does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, precise location, advertising identifier, or the contents of your own documents for analytics. Amplitude may process technical information, such as device or network information, as part of providing its service. See Amplitude’s Privacy Notice for details.

Your consent and choices

Analytics is off by default. You can enable or disable Share Anonymous Usage at any time in ADHD Reader’s Settings screen. Disabling it withdraws your consent and stops ADHD Reader from sending new analytics events. ADHD Reader’s core and paid features remain available whether or not you consent to analytics.

How information is shared

ADHD Reader shares purchase-related information with Apple and, only with your consent, usage analytics with Amplitude as needed to provide, maintain, and improve the app. We require service providers that receive information from ADHD Reader to protect it consistently with this policy and applicable privacy requirements. ADHD Reader does not sell personal information and does not include third-party advertising or use information for cross-app tracking.

Retention and deletion

Reading progress and settings remain on your device until you clear them in ADHD Reader or remove the app. Removing ADHD Reader deletes its locally stored data from that device. Analytics already sent to Amplitude is retained only as long as reasonably needed to analyze and improve ADHD Reader, after which it is deleted or de-identified.

You may request deletion of analytics information associated with your installation by emailing spencerhall31@gmail.com. Because ADHD Reader does not create an account or collect your identity, we may need information from your installation to locate responsive records, and in some cases may not be able to associate anonymous records with you.

Children’s privacy

ADHD Reader does not require an account and does not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

Security

ADHD Reader limits information collection to what is used to provide and improve the app and relies on Apple’s and its service providers’ safeguards. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when ADHD Reader’s features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, email Spencer Hall at spencerhall31@gmail.com.
